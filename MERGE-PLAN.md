# FinishKit merge plan

FinishKit replaces the 4- and 8-bar arrangement desks published in `workinwithai-create` from 2026-09-05 to 2026-10-26 with one "finish my song" app. Nothing in the old repos or deploys was changed, archived or deleted.

Excluded (standalone tools, untouched and not merged): WordFour, PorchBand, EarFour, HumEight, CHORDLOCK, TuneKit, BoxFour, TapFour, SweepKit, DoubleFour, Patchform, HookGrid, RefPunch, MaskClaim, ZeroBar.

**137 desks catalogued → 38 moves in 5 categories.** 136 are merged (133 as a move preset, 3 absorbed into the timeline or the per-move sound options); 1 is not merged (ColdNote, see the end).

## How near-duplicates were folded

Desks that did the same job at a different length or with a different instrument became one move, and the difference became an option (bars, sound, pattern). Each old desk name loads its preset: type the name into **Browse all moves** in the app.

## Not merged, and why

- **ColdNote**: it finds sour notes in your own loop. That's analysis, not an arrangement move, so it stays its own desk.
- **FormCut, Inkwell** (arrangement maps): no single move fits, so FinishKit's timeline does the job (Auto-arrange, sections, locks).
- **FitFour** (instrument fit): absorbed as the Sound option on every move, plus the mixer.
- Voices the pinned sample sets don't have were mapped to the closest real sample, and the move's sound label says what plays: congas/bongos (CongaFour → rim/clave pocket + shaker), clap (ClapFour → side-stick/snare), trombone/tenor (StabFour → trumpet/horn). ShakeFour's old "shaker" was really agogo voices; it is replaced by a real shaker and tambourine (VSCO 2 CE, CC0).
